## SET_10｜節奏拆解・低音延展・仍在低鳴至完全靜默 (Outro｜Decay & Dissolve ➔ [ "Silence" ])

- **編曲導引標籤（Arrangement Directive Tag）：** `[ "Outro: Decay and dissolve. Pull the rhythm apart, let the low tones stretch, and let the last echoes thin out to nothing." ] ➔ [ "Silence" ]`
